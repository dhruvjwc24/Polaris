# Polaris — Mockup Templates

## Product Purpose

Generate custom landing page mockups for local service businesses (roofers, plumbers, HVAC, landscapers, salons, law firms) to use in cold outreach. The prospect receives a link to a working, branded, custom-built website mockup with their actual business name, reviews, and photos — before any sales conversation happens. The decision shifts from "should we build a website?" to "do you want THIS specific website?"

## Register

brand — the mockup IS the product. It has to stop a business owner mid-scroll and make them think "this is mine."

## Users

Local business owners. Non-technical. 40–65. Operated their business offline for years. Skeptical of generic proposals. Respond to specificity — if the mockup has their actual Google reviews, their actual business name styled well, their actual location, they pay attention.

## Brand Voice

Earned trust. Grounded. Like a referral from a neighbor, not an agency pitch deck. Warm but competent. The opposite of corporate.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient hero, Inter, "Trusted by 10,000+ businesses")
- AI-obvious templates (purple gradients, Playfair Display headlines, "Welcome to" copy)
- Cheap local business templates (WordPressish, Comic Sans energy, tacky stock photos)
- Overly minimal agency aesthetic (maximally whitespace, unreadable on a phone screen)

## Design Principles

1. The business feels REAL — their name, their location, their reviews, their phone number visible and prominent
2. Niche-appropriate aesthetic — a roofing company should not look like a tech startup
3. Mobile-first — most prospects open on iPhone, often in the field
4. Credibility signals front and center — star ratings, review count, real quotes
5. One clear action — call/text/book now; no confusion about next step
6. Photography-forward when available — real Google Maps photos outperform any stock image

## Strategic Principles

- The mockup is a cold email attachment replacement. It has 3 seconds to earn a longer look.
- Niche dictates aesthetic. Roofers: rugged, direct, trustworthy. Salons: warm, editorial, soft. HVAC: dependable, utility, urgency. Law firms: authoritative, traditional, precise.
- Real data wins. If the business has 4.9 stars and 87 reviews, that must be the hero element.
- The prospect has seen a thousand generic websites. Make theirs feel earned.
